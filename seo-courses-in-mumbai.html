What you'll learn

Updated for 2026. Tap a module to see its topics.

01On-page SEO
  • How search engines crawl, index and rank
  • Keyword research, search intent and keyword clustering
  • Competitor keyword analysis
  • Title tags, meta descriptions, headings (H1–H6)
  • URL structure and internal linking
  • Image names and alt text
  • Writing content that ranks
  • Schema markup (structured data)
  • AI-assisted content briefs and meta titles
02Technical SEO & Google Search Console
  • Search Console setup and performance reports
  • Indexing, XML sitemaps and robots.txt
  • Canonical tags and redirects (301/302)
  • Core Web Vitals and page speed
  • Mobile-friendliness
  • Duplicate content and how to fix it
03Off-page & local SEO
  • Link building methods that are safe in 2026
  • Guest posts, PR and digital mentions
  • Local SEO with Google Business Profile
  • Local citations and reviews
  • Backlink audits
  • AI-assisted outreach emails
04YouTube SEO
  • YouTube keyword research
  • Titles, descriptions, tags and chapters
  • Thumbnails and audience retention
  • YouTube Analytics
  • AI-generated titles and descriptions from transcripts
05Amazon SEO
  • How Amazon search ranks products
  • Amazon keyword research
  • Listing titles, bullets and images
  • Reviews and ranking factors
  • AI-written and A/B-tested listing copy
06AI SEO: GEO & AEOAI
  • Generative Engine Optimisation (GEO) and Answer Engine Optimisation (AEO)
  • Google AI Overviews vs AI Mode
  • How ChatGPT, Perplexity and Gemini choose sources
  • Schema and entities for AI citations
  • llms.txt and robots.txt for AI crawlers
  • Tracking AI search visibility
  • Fact-checking AI content for E-E-A-T
07SEO tools & reporting
  • Google Search Console and GA4
  • Semrush and Ahrefs Webmaster Tools
  • Screaming Frog site audits
  • Rank tracking
  • Monthly SEO reports for clients or managers

Frequently asked questions

Want the full digital marketing skill set? See the Advanced Diploma in Digital Marketing with AI.

Do I need my own website?

No. We give you a practice website to work on. If you have your own site or a business website, you can use that for your project.

Does the course cover AI tools for SEO?

Yes. AI is used in every module, and there's a full module on AI search optimisation (GEO and AEO) at no extra cost.

What is the fee for the SEO course?

₹12,000, including the AI SEO module. SEO is also part of the Advanced Diploma in Digital Marketing with AI (₹30,000).

Can I learn SEO online?

Yes, as live online classes with the same trainer and syllabus. Classroom batches run at Kemps Corner and Bandra West.

Will SEO still matter with AI search?

Yes. AI Overviews and assistants like ChatGPT pull from pages that are well structured, trusted and indexed, which is exactly what SEO builds. The course teaches both.
