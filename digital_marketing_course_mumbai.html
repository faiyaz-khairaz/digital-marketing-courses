How long is the Advanced Diploma in Digital Marketing?

60 sessions, which most students complete in about 3 months. Weekday, weekend and fast-track batches are available, and you choose timings that fit your work or college.

What is the fee?

The Advanced Diploma in Digital Marketing with AI is ₹30,000. Combined programmes with web design, graphic design or MIS start at ₹38,000. See the full list on the fees page.

Can I do the course online?

Yes. Every course is available as live, instructor-led online classes with the same trainer, syllabus, projects and certificate as the classroom batches at Kemps Corner and Bandra West.

Do I need a marketing or technical background?

No. The course starts from the basics. Students, freshers, working professionals, business owners and homemakers all join. Basic computer and internet use is enough.

Which certifications will I get?

You receive the Compufield diploma certificate, and we prepare you for Google's free Skillshop certifications, including Google Ads Search, Display, Video and Shopping, and Google Analytics, plus other industry certifications covered in class.

Is placement guaranteed?

We provide internship support, live projects, interview preparation and placement assistance. Our students work at companies such as Swiggy, ESPN, Fastrack, Ultra Media and 24 Frames Digital. Your result depends on your effort and portfolio, so we focus on making you job-ready.

How is AI covered?

AI is part of every module, not a separate add-on. You use ChatGPT, Gemini, Canva AI, Adobe Firefly and Google's AI-powered ads and analytics features for keyword research, content, creatives, ad copy and reporting.
